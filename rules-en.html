H0C RULES

The full alliance document: ranks, duties and standards.

← BACK TO H0C
I. PURPOSE AND PRINCIPLES⌄

H0C is built on teamwork, communication, mutual respect, determination and shared commitment to the success of the alliance.

Alliance ranks are not merely titles. Each rank represents a level of trust, responsibility, activity and commitment to alliance life.

H0C strength depends not only on the strongest accounts, but also on our ability to cooperate, communicate effectively, participate in alliance activities and support one another.

All members and Officers are expected to:

  • Treat alliance members and other alliances with respect.
  • Follow alliance rules and decisions.
  • Pass on important information promptly.
  • Participate in alliance activities and events as far as possible.
  • Support rallies, reinforcements and military operations when required.
  • Follow instructions during coordinated events.
  • Help new and developing members whenever possible.
  • Avoid unnecessary arguments, drama and conflicts.
  • Resolve misunderstandings privately and professionally when possible.
  • Put the interests of H0C above personal rank or benefit.
No member or Officer stands above the alliance.
II. OFFICERS — GENERAL EXPECTATIONS⌄

An Officer position is a position of trust and responsibility, not a privilege. Officers help the alliance function effectively and maintain a positive, well-organized atmosphere.

All Officers are expected to:

  • Maintain high activity and regular presence in the game.
  • Take responsibility for assigned duties.
  • Work with the Leader and other Officers.
  • Pass important information to the appropriate Officers.
  • Answer members’ questions, concerns and problems.
  • Show initiative instead of waiting for instructions on every matter.
  • Remain calm and professional during misunderstandings or conflicts.
  • Support alliance decisions once they are made.
  • Protect H0C interests and reputation.
  • Avoid using rank for personal benefit.
  • Maintain appropriate confidentiality regarding internal matters.
  • Support other Officers when necessary.

An Officer who cannot perform their duties for justified reasons should inform the Leader so the duties can be temporarily or permanently transferred.

III. FINANCIAL ADVISOR⌄

The Financial Advisor helps oversee alliance finances, investments and resource matters.

Duties

  • Monitor the use of alliance resources.
  • Promote responsible resource management.
  • Monitor alliance spending, investments and rewards.
  • Identify current and future resource needs.
  • Advise the Leader and Officers on resource priorities.
  • Recommend efficient ways to use alliance resources.
  • Keep appropriate records when necessary.
  • Help ensure resources are used for the benefit of the whole H0C alliance.
Key qualities: responsibility, honesty, organization, financial awareness and sound decision-making.
IV. DIPLOMACY ADVISOR⌄

The Diplomacy Advisor manages and maintains H0C relations with other alliances and important players.

Duties

  • Maintain professional communication with other alliances.
  • Represent H0C respectfully and professionally.
  • Negotiate agreements when properly authorized.
  • Monitor developments in the diplomatic situation.
  • Inform the Leader and Officers about important external developments.
  • Help prevent unnecessary conflicts.
  • Respond calmly and professionally to provocations.
  • Assist with NAPs, agreements, territory arrangements and other diplomatic matters according to instructions.
  • Help protect and strengthen H0C reputation.

Authority

The Diplomacy Advisor may not independently declare war, break important agreements or commit H0C to significant diplomatic arrangements without consultation with the Leader and/or appropriate Officers.

V. WAREHOUSE MASTER I⌄

Warehouse Master I is responsible for the day-to-day management and organization of alliance resources.

Duties

  • Monitor available alliance resources.
  • Monitor current and expected alliance needs.
  • Keep the warehouse organized.
  • Carry out approved resource distribution decisions.
  • Track significant shortages or resource demand.
  • Pass important resource information to Officers.
  • Ensure resources are distributed according to alliance priorities.
  • Protect resources from unnecessary or unauthorized use.

Warehouse Master I is primarily responsible for the daily operation of the warehouse.

VI. WAREHOUSE MASTER II⌄

Warehouse Master II is the main support and deputy to Warehouse Master I.

Duties

  • Help monitor alliance resources.
  • Maintain appropriate records and information.
  • Help distribute resources.
  • Monitor members’ needs.
  • Support warehouse organization.
  • Work closely with Warehouse Master I.
  • Take over duties when Warehouse Master I is absent.
Warehouse Masters I and II should operate as a coordinated team.
VII. DEFENDER I⌄

Defender I is responsible for defensive readiness and coordination within the alliance.

Duties

  • Monitor threats to alliance territory and members.
  • Coordinate defensive actions.
  • Organize reinforcements when needed.
  • Inform appropriate Officers and members about potential threats.
  • Coordinate defensive responses during alliance events.
  • Monitor the battlefield during major events.
  • Work with the Lord Commander during military operations.
  • Ensure members understand defensive instructions.
  • Help respond to emergencies requiring immediate action.
VIII. DEFENDER II⌄

Defender II supports Defender I and serves as their main deputy.

Duties

  • Help organize defensive actions.
  • Monitor the battlefield and alliance territory.
  • Pass important information to Officers and members.
  • Coordinate member support and reinforcements.
  • Help with emergency defensive actions.
  • Replace Defender I when necessary.
Defenders I and II should operate as a coordinated team.
IX. LORD COMMANDER⌄

The Lord Commander coordinates H0C military operations. The role is primarily that of strategist, organizer and communicator. It does not have to belong to the strongest player in the alliance.

Duties

  • Develop military strategies.
  • Plan and coordinate alliance attacks.
  • Organize rallies and coordinated attacks.
  • Coordinate reinforcements and troop movement.
  • Organize H0C participation in Lost Realm, KvK, Siege of Winterfell, Alliance Conquest and other major military events.
  • Give clear instructions before and during events.
  • Work with the Defenders.
  • Analyze the battlefield.
  • Adapt strategy to changing circumstances.
  • Make appropriately fast decisions when immediate action is required.
  • Consult major strategic decisions with the Leader and Officers.
  • Help members understand their individual roles during coordinated operations.

Chain of command during military operations

During organized military events, members should follow the Lord Commander or designated event commander. If a member disagrees with a military decision, it should be discussed before or after the operation unless the Commander explicitly asks for input during the operation.

The goal during an active operation is coordinated execution, not multiple competing strategies.

X. EXPECTATIONS DURING MAJOR EVENTS⌄

Major GOTWIC events require much more coordination than normal alliance activity. Members taking part in Lost Realm, KvK, Siege of Winterfell and Alliance Conquest should:

  • Read and understand event instructions.
  • Be available during designated periods whenever possible.
  • Follow rally and reinforcement instructions.
  • Use required troops and commanders for specified objectives when instructed.
  • Avoid independent action if it could disrupt the alliance operation.
  • Inform leadership if they cannot complete an assigned task.
  • Save troops when required.
  • Follow designated objectives and attack priorities.
  • Avoid unnecessary losses from unauthorized attacks.
  • Reinforce alliance objectives when required.
  • Stay attentive to alliance communications during active operations.
During major events, individual results should not come at the expense of the alliance objective unless the event commander explicitly permits it.
XI. COMMUNICATION⌄

Effective communication is essential to alliance success.

Members should:

  • Check alliance messages and announcements regularly.
  • Read event instructions before joining.
  • Respond when Officers request confirmation or information.
  • Inform Officers if they will be unavailable for an extended period.
  • Ask questions when instructions are unclear.
  • Avoid flooding alliance communication channels with unnecessary arguments or private disputes.

Officers should communicate important information clearly, accurately and on time.

Instructions for major events should state:

  1. The objective.
  2. The person responsible.
  3. The start time.
  4. What members should do.
  5. What members should not do.
XII. MEMBER RANKS — MARQUIS⌄

Role: an active and trusted leader among members. A Marquis is not an Officer, but should be someone the alliance can rely on.

Expectations

  • Maintain high activity.
  • Regularly participate in alliance events.
  • Join and reinforce rallies.
  • Help other members.
  • Support new and developing players.
  • Show initiative.
  • Set a positive example.
  • Work with Officers.
  • Report important problems or situations.
  • Help maintain a positive alliance atmosphere.
  • Be ready to take on additional duties when needed.

A Marquis should show qualities expected of a potential future Officer, but promotion to Officer is not automatic.

XIII. COUNT⌄

Role: an active and engaged member who regularly contributes to alliance life.

Expectations

  • Maintain regular activity.
  • Participate in alliance events.
  • Join and reinforce rallies.
  • Support other members.
  • Follow alliance rules and instructions.
  • Work with other members.
  • Maintain a positive and respectful attitude.
  • Show increasing initiative and responsibility.

A Count may be considered for promotion to Marquis after consistently demonstrating greater activity, initiative, reliability and commitment.

XIV. BARON⌄

Role: an active member beginning the path toward greater involvement in alliance life.

Expectations

  • Maintain regular activity.
  • Develop their account.
  • Participate in alliance activities.
  • Follow alliance rules.
  • Support alliance activities.
  • Work with other members.
  • Learn alliance procedures and event rules.
  • Show willingness to become more involved.
XV. PROMOTION RULES⌄

Rank is not a reward based solely on account strength.

Rank reflects:

  • Activity.
  • Reliability.
  • Participation.
  • Teamwork.
  • Initiative.
  • Responsibility.
  • Communication.
  • Trust.
  • Contribution to alliance development.

Normal promotion path: Baron → Count → Marquis → Officer.

Promotion is not automatic and not every Marquis must or should become an Officer.

Officer role selection

  • Financial Advisor: responsible, trustworthy, resource-aware.
  • Diplomacy Advisor: calm, communicative, professional and diplomatic.
  • Warehouse Master: organized, systematic, accurate and trustworthy.
  • Defender: alert, active, quick to respond and able to work under pressure.
  • Lord Commander: strategic, decisive, organized and able to communicate clearly.
The higher the rank, the greater the responsibility toward the entire alliance.
XVI. INACTIVITY AND ABSENCE⌄

Private life always comes first, and members are not expected to be constantly available in the game. However, prolonged inactivity can affect alliance operations.

  • Members expecting a longer absence should, where possible, inform an Officer.
  • Officers should inform leadership about planned absences so their duties can be temporarily reassigned.

Long-term unexplained inactivity may result in:

  • Temporary transfer of duties.
  • Rank reduction.
  • Removal from event assignments.
  • Removal from the alliance if necessary to preserve alliance effectiveness.

The purpose is not punishment. It is to ensure H0C can plan around actual member availability.

XVII. CONFLICT RESOLUTION⌄

Misunderstandings are unavoidable in any alliance, especially during competition and events.

Members and Officers are expected to:

  • Resolve misunderstandings respectfully.
  • Avoid personal attacks.
  • Avoid public arguments that harm alliance morale.
  • Pass serious disputes to the appropriate Officer or Leader.
  • Respect decisions once they are final.
  • Avoid retaliation against alliance members or outside players without proper authorization.

Officers should seek to resolve conflicts fairly, privately and professionally.

XVIII. USE OF AUTHORITY⌄

Ranks exist to serve the alliance.

No Officer or member may use their position to:

  • Gain personal benefits at the alliance’s expense.
  • Misuse alliance resources.
  • Intimidate or harass other members.
  • Make unauthorized diplomatic commitments.
  • Create unnecessary conflicts.
  • Bypass established procedures for personal reasons.

Authority should always be used fairly, transparently and in the best interests of H0C.

XIX. ONE ALLIANCE — ONE TEAM⌄

H0C succeeds when its members act together.

Strong accounts are valuable, but coordinated teamwork allows the alliance to compete effectively during major GOTWIC events.

Every member has a role. Every contribution matters.

The goal is not simply to have the strongest players. The goal is to build an alliance that is organized, reliable, competitive, respectful and enjoyable to be part of.

Rank is earned through trust.
Leadership is shown through service to others.
Victory comes through teamwork.
H0C — ONE ALLIANCE. ONE TEAM.
